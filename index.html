Am I a Candidate?

Not everyone is eligible — and we'll tell you honestly.

A licensed physician makes the final call after reviewing your health history. Some people should not take GLP-1 or peptide therapy.

Check Your Eligibility
  • Generally considered for adults with a BMI in the overweight/obese range, or weight-related health conditions
  • Not appropriate during pregnancy or while breastfeeding
  • Not appropriate with a personal/family history of certain thyroid cancers
