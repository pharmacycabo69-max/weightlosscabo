Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy explains what information WeightLossCabo collects through this website, how it's used, and the choices you have. It covers this website only — it is separate from any clinical or medical records created if you become a patient, which are governed by applicable healthcare privacy law and our clinic's internal patient-privacy practices.

Information we collect

When you submit our consultation or contact forms, we collect what you provide directly: name, email, phone number, and any notes or health-related interests you choose to share. We do not require this information to browse the site, and no information is collected from casual visitors beyond standard, non-identifying technical data (such as pages viewed) if analytics are enabled.

How we use it

Form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule or discuss an evaluation, and provide information about our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Eligibility Concierge tool

Our self-guided eligibility questionnaire on the Am I a Candidate? page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not transmitted to us, stored, or logged anywhere — they exist only on your device for the duration of your session and are cleared when you leave or refresh the page.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use standard analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns (for example, which pages are visited). Where used, these tools do not receive the contents of any form you submit. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Data retention

We retain form submissions only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, after which they are deleted or archived securely.

Your choices

You may request a copy of the information you've submitted to us, ask us to correct it, or request its deletion, subject to any records we're required to keep for legal or healthcare-recordkeeping purposes. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@weightlosscabo.com or +52 624 226 1023.
