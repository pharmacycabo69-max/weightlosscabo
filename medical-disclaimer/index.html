Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This website is informational only

Everything on WeightLossCabo — including the Home, How It Works, Treatments, Pricing, Eligibility, About, FAQ, and Blog pages — is provided for general educational purposes only. It is not medical advice, is not a substitute for professional medical judgment, and should not be used to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

No physician-patient relationship

Browsing this site, using the Eligibility Concierge self-check tool, or submitting a consultation request form does not create a physician-patient relationship. That relationship begins only once you have completed an in-person evaluation with one of our licensed physicians at our Los Cabos clinic.

Prescription required — this is not a pharmacy storefront

GLP-1 medications and the peptides listed in our Treatments catalogue are prescription therapies. Prices shown on this site are for informational reference only; nothing can be purchased, ordered, or dispensed through this website. Every treatment requires an in-person medical evaluation, a valid prescription from a licensed physician, and dispensing through our affiliated pharmacy.

Not everyone is a candidate

These medications are not appropriate for everyone. They are generally contraindicated for people who are pregnant or breastfeeding, and for anyone with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2). Additional contraindications and precautions apply depending on the specific medication — see the relevant Treatments page and, most importantly, discuss your full health history with your physician. Only a licensed physician who has evaluated you in person can determine whether you're a candidate for any treatment discussed on this site.

Results are not guaranteed

We do not promise, guarantee, or imply any specific outcome, weight-loss amount, or timeline. Individual results vary based on many factors, including health history, adherence, and individual physiology. Any figures, testimonials, or examples referenced on this site (where present) are illustrative, not a promise of your results.

Risks and side effects

GLP-1 medications and other peptides carry real risks and potential side effects, ranging from common and mild (nausea, digestive changes, injection-site reactions) to rare and serious (pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, allergic reactions, and — per FDA boxed warnings for GLP-1 medications — a risk of thyroid C-cell tumors observed in rodent studies, of uncertain relevance to humans). Your physician will review the specific risk profile of any recommended treatment during your evaluation. We encourage you to also review FDA prescribing information directly; relevant sources are linked from our Blog articles.

In case of emergency

This website, its forms, and its chatbot-style tools are not monitored for emergencies and are not a substitute for emergency care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.
