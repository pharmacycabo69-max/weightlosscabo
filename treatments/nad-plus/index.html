NAD+

A coenzyme involved in cellular energy production, offered as IV or injectable therapy for recovery and vitality support.

How it works

Replenishes NAD+ levels, a coenzyme that naturally declines with age and is involved in mitochondrial energy production.

Who it's typically considered for

Adults seeking recovery, energy, or general wellness support, evaluated individually.

Common side effects

  • — Flushing
  • — Nausea during infusion
  • — Injection-site reactions

Who should not take this

  • — Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • — Severe kidney disease
