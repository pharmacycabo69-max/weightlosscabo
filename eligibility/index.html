Eligibility

Not everyone is eligible — and we'll tell you honestly.

A licensed physician makes the final determination after an in-person evaluation. The list below covers general criteria — it's not exhaustive and isn't a substitute for that evaluation.

Generally considered for

  • — Adults 18 and older
  • — A BMI in the overweight or obese range, or weight-related health conditions (for GLP-1 therapy)
  • — General good health with no contraindicated conditions

Generally not appropriate for

  • — Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • — A personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2 (for GLP-1 therapy)
  • — Active untreated malignancy (for most peptide therapies)
  • — Known hypersensitivity to the specific medication
