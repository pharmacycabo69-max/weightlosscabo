Peptide Therapy in Los Cabos: How Our Walk-In-to-Delivery Process Works

If you’re researching GLP-1 or peptide therapy while in or traveling to Los Cabos, it helps to understand how care is actually structured here — because it’s genuinely different from ordering something online.

Step 1: In-person evaluation, always first

Nothing is prescribed, sold, or shipped before a licensed physician evaluates you in person. That visit covers your health history, current medications, relevant labs, and — critically — screens for the conditions that make GLP-1 or dual-agonist therapy inappropriate, such as a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, MEN 2, or pregnancy. This isn’t a formality; it’s the step that determines whether treatment is appropriate for you at all.

Step 2: A licensed prescription, not a catalogue order

Our treatments catalogue lists the medications we work with and displays representative pricing so you know what to expect financially — but it is explicitly not a storefront. You can’t add a peptide to a cart and check out. Every product on that page requires a physician’s evaluation and prescription first. Displaying prices without a purchase flow is a deliberate choice: we want you informed before you visit, not transacting before you’ve been evaluated.

Step 3: Our affiliated pharmacy dispenses and delivers refills

Once you have a prescription, our affiliated pharmacy fills it. For ongoing treatment, refills can be delivered rather than requiring a pharmacy visit every time — but refill delivery still follows your physician’s active prescription and monitoring schedule. It’s a convenience layer on top of clinical supervision, not a replacement for it.

Step 4: Telehealth for follow-up, not for your first visit

After the initial in-person evaluation, routine follow-ups can often happen via telehealth — reviewing how you’re tolerating the medication, adjusting dosing, and renewing prescriptions as appropriate. This hybrid model is built for people who split time between Los Cabos and elsewhere, or who simply want fewer required office visits once a treatment plan is established.

Why we built it this way

None of this is regulatory box-checking for its own sake. GLP-1 and peptide medications carry real contraindications and side effects, and a physician who has actually examined you is the only person positioned to say whether a given medication is appropriate for your situation. If you’re not sure you qualify, our Eligibility page has a private, non-binding self-check to help you think it through before you book.
